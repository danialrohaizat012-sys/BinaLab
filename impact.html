How we measure

No vanity metrics.

Our reporting model follows a clear path: application → eligibility review → participation or project delivery → evidence collection → independent administrative verification → approved public aggregate.

What counts?

Youth trained means unique participants with verified attendance. Businesses digitalised means a solution delivered and accepted, not simply an application. Completed projects require a completion record. Countries reached represent verified participant locations, not web traffic.

Public reporting excludes participant contact details, confidential business information and identifiable records without consent. Small groups may be suppressed to protect privacy.

Data source: approved aggregate records from BinaLab Studio (integration planned). Dashboard displays no statistics until a verified public feed is available.
