Our story

Started with systems. Now starting with your website.

BinaLab began by building business systems for local SMEs: booking, inventory and operations tools for car rental, F&B, retail and entertainment businesses. Working closely with owners, we saw the same thing again and again: good businesses that looked smaller online than they really were.

Today a website is usually the first thing a customer checks. So that's where we start. We build websites that explain clearly what a business does, earn trust quickly and make it easy to get in touch.

When a business is ready for more, we build the systems behind the website too. One partner, from first impression to day-to-day operations.
