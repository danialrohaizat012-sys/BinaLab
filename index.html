FAQ

Questions clients usually ask.

Do you work with clients outside Malaysia?

Yes. We work remotely over WhatsApp, email and video calls, and plan check-ins around your time zone.

How much does a website cost?

It depends on the number of pages, features and how much content you already have. Send us a short description of your business and what you need, and we'll reply with a clear quote and timeline.

How long does it take?

It depends on the scope and how quickly content is ready. You'll get a timeline with your quote, and we agree milestones before work starts.

Do I need to have my text and photos ready?

It helps, but it isn't required. We can help you plan your pages and polish your text, and suggest what photos you'll need.

Can you build a booking, order or inventory system too?

Yes. Business systems are where BinaLab started. We can build one alongside your website or add it later.

What happens after the website is live?

We walk you through it and stay available for updates and improvements. You can keep working with us or manage the site yourself.
